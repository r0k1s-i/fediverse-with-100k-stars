Fediverse with 100k Stars is a 3D WebGL visualization of the Fediverse, rendering instances as stars in a galaxy.

Zoom in to explore instances, labels, and close-range GLB planet models. Use mouse or touch to pan and zoom.

Based on Chrome Experiments: 100,000 Stars.

Source Code: https://github.com/r0k1s-i/fediverse-with-100k-stars
